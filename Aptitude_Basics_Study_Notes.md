# 📘 APTITUDE BASICS – STUDY NOTES

## 📌 Overview
This document contains detailed formulas, short tricks, and concept notes for all Basic Aptitude topics. Follow the sequence step-by-step to build a strong foundation for interview preparation.

---

## 🧠 1. NUMBER SYSTEM
### 📘 Concepts:
- Types of numbers: Natural, Whole, Integers
- Prime and Composite numbers
- Factors and multiples
- Divisibility rules

### 📌 Important Points:
- Prime number: divisible only by 1 and itself
- Even numbers always divisible by 2

### ⚡ Short Tricks:
- **Divisible by 2** → last digit even
- **Divisible by 3** → sum of digits divisible by 3
- **Divisible by 9** → sum of digits divisible by 9

---

## 📊 2. SIMPLIFICATION
### 📘 Concepts:
- **BODMAS rule** (Bracket, Order, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction)
- Fractions and decimals

### 📌 Key Rule:
- Always solve in correct BODMAS order

### ⚡ Short Tricks:
- Convert fractions to decimals for fast solving
- Use approximation when options are close

---

## 📈 3. PERCENTAGES
### 📘 Concepts:
- Percentage meaning and conversion
- Increase and decrease problems

### 📌 Formulas:
- `% = (Value / Total) × 100`
- `Increase % = [(New - Old) / Old] × 100`
- `Decrease % = [(Old - New) / Old] × 100`

### ⚡ Short Tricks:
- **10%** = divide by 10
- **20%** = divide by 5
- **25%** = divide by 4
- **50%** = half

---

## 🔢 4. RATIO & PROPORTION
### 📘 Concepts:
- Ratio comparison between quantities
- Direct and inverse proportion
- `a:b = a/b`
- If `a:b = c:d` → `ad = bc`

### ⚡ Short Tricks:
- Always simplify ratio first
- Cross multiplication saves time

---

## 📊 5. AVERAGES
### 📘 Concepts:
- Mean of a set of numbers
- Weighted average basics

### 📌 Formula:
- `Average = Sum of values / Number of values`

### ⚡ Short Tricks:
- Assume average to reduce calculations
- Use deviation method for faster solving

---

## 💰 6. SIMPLE INTEREST
### 📘 Concepts:
- Interest on principal amount

### 📌 Formula:
- `SI = (P × R × T) / 100`

### ⚡ Short Tricks:
- **10%** of any number is easiest to calculate first
- Break large numbers into simple parts

---

## 💵 7. COMPOUND INTEREST
### 📘 Concepts:
- Interest on principal + accumulated interest

### 📌 Formula:
- `CI = P(1 + R/100)^T - P`

### ⚡ Short Tricks:
- For 2 years use: `CI ≈ SI + extra interest`
- Use approximation for quick solving

---

## 🚀 ROADMAP SEQUENCE
**Number System** → **Simplification** → **Percentages** → **Ratio & Proportion** → **Averages** → **Simple Interest** → **Compound Interest**

---

- Practice 20–30 questions daily
- Focus on speed + accuracy
