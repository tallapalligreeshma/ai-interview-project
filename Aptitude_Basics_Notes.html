📊 2. Simplification
📘 Concepts

Follow the BODMAS rule: Bracket, Order, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction.

Short Trick: Convert fractions to decimals for faster mental solving.

📈 3. Percentages
📌 Formulas

% = (Value / Total) × 100

Increase % = [(New - Old) / Old] × 100

⚡ Short Tricks

10% = Div by 10 | 20% = Div by 5 | 25% = Div by 4

🔢 4. Ratio & Proportion

Relationship between two quantities: a:b = a/b

If a:b = c:d, then ad = bc (Cross Multiplication).

📊 5. Averages

Mean of a set of values.

Formula: Sum of values / Number of values

Short Trick: Use the Deviation Method to assume an average and adjust instead of full summation.

💰 6. Simple Interest (SI)

Interest calculated only on the principal amount.

Formula: SI = (P × R × T) / 100

💵 7. Compound Interest (CI)

Interest calculated on principal and accumulated interest.

Formula: CI = P(1 + R/100)^T - P

Short Trick: Use CI ≈ SI + extra interest for 2-year problems.
